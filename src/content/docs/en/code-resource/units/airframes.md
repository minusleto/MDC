## Key

- `small_plane` — light aircraft · `medium_plane` — medium aircraft · `large_plane` — heavy aircraft
- `cv_` — carrier-based
- `cas` — Close Air Support · `awacs` — AWACS · `scout` — reconnaissance · `strike` — strike · `suicide` — kamikaze drone
