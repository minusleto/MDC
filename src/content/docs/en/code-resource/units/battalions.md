## Prefix key

- `L_` — light · `Mot_` — motorized · `Mech_` — mechanized · `Arm_` — armored
- `armor_` — tank · `SP_` — self-propelled · `HQ_` — unit headquarters
- `Bat` — battalion · `Comp` — company · `Battery` — battery
- `Air_Inf` — air infantry · `Recce` — reconnaissance · `Arty` — artillery · `R_Arty` — rocket artillery
