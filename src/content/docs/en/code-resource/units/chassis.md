## Chassis

| Token | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `medium_tank_amphibious_chassis` | Amphibious chassis based on a medium tank |
| `medium_tank_aa_chassis` | SPAAG chassis based on a medium tank |
| `medium_tank_artillery_chassis` | SPG chassis based on a medium tank |
| `medium_tank_destroyer_chassis` | Tank destroyer chassis based on a medium tank |
| `medium_tank_flame_chassis` | Flame tank chassis based on a medium tank |
| `heavy_tank_amphibious_chassis` | Amphibious chassis based on a heavy tank |
